# Curriculum Structure


## 4.1 Curriculum Organisation


The Artificial Intelligence Curriculum Framework is organised around
four interconnected domains that collectively support the development of
AI literacy, computational thinking, ethical awareness, and practical
problem-solving.

These domains provide a coherent structure through which learners
progressively develop knowledge, understanding, skills, and dispositions
related to artificial intelligence.

Each domain is revisited throughout Years 3–9 through a spiral
progression model, enabling learners to deepen their understanding while
applying previously acquired knowledge in increasingly complex contexts.

### AI Recognition & Understanding (RU)


This domain focuses on developing learners’ conceptual understanding of
artificial intelligence systems.

Learners explore how AI technologies function, how they process
information, and how they differ from traditional rule-based systems.

Progression within this domain supports learners in moving from simple
recognition of AI technologies towards analysing system architectures,
evaluating capabilities, and understanding limitations.

Key themes include:

- recognising AI in everyday life;

- understanding inputs, processes, and outputs;

- AI system behaviour;

- AI applications;

- AI capabilities and limitations;

- intelligent system design.

### Machine Learning & Data (ML)


This domain focuses on understanding how AI systems learn from data.

Learners investigate how machine learning models identify patterns, make
predictions, and improve performance through training and evaluation.

Learning experiences progressively introduce concepts related to data
quality, classification, model accuracy, testing, and optimisation.

Key themes include:

- pattern recognition;

- classification;

- datasets;

- training and testing;

- accuracy;

- evaluation;

- data analysis;

- machine learning workflows.

### Prompt Engineering & AI Interaction (PE)


This domain focuses on effective communication and interaction with AI
systems.

Learners explore how instructions influence AI behaviour and develop the
ability to design, refine, and evaluate prompts for different purposes.

Progression supports increasingly sophisticated interactions with
generative AI systems and AI-assisted workflows.

Key themes include:

- instruction design;

- prompt construction;

- prompt refinement;

- AI-assisted problem-solving;

- workflow design;

- AI-supported research and creativity.

### Ethics & Societal Impact (ES)


This domain focuses on the responsible development and use of artificial
intelligence.

Learners investigate ethical considerations, societal implications, and
the broader impact of AI technologies on individuals and communities.

Ethical understanding is embedded throughout the curriculum rather than
delivered as a separate unit.

Key themes include:

- fairness;

- bias;

- privacy;

- transparency;

- accountability;

- misinformation;

- intellectual property;

- responsible innovation.

## 4.2 Learning Cycle


All learning experiences within the curriculum are structured around a
common inquiry-based learning cycle.

This model promotes active engagement, reflection, and iterative
improvement while supporting the development of critical thinking and
problem-solving skills.

### Explore


Learners encounter a concept, problem, or AI application and begin
developing initial understanding through observation, discussion, and
investigation.

### Interact


Learners engage directly with AI systems, tools, datasets, or scenarios
and begin applying new knowledge.

### Test


Learners evaluate outputs, investigate system behaviour, and analyse
outcomes.

### Refine


Learners improve prompts, datasets, solutions, or approaches based on
evidence and feedback.

### Reflect


Learners consider ethical implications, evaluate effectiveness, and
identify opportunities for further improvement.

## 4.3 Curriculum Delivery Model

The curriculum is designed to be delivered through:

| Component            | Allocation                              |
|----------------------|-----------------------------------------|
| Lessons per Year     | 10                                      |
| Lesson Duration      | 90 Minutes                              |
| Annual Teaching Time | 15 Hours                                |
| Curriculum Model     | Project-Based and Inquiry-Based         |
| Assessment Approach  | Continuous Assessment and Final Project |

## 4.4 Unit Structure

Each year group is organised into five thematic units.

| Unit   | Focus                     |
|--------|---------------------------|
| Unit 1 | Understanding AI Systems  |
| Unit 2 | Data and Machine Learning |
| Unit 3 | Interacting with AI       |
| Unit 4 | Evaluating AI Systems     |
| Unit 5 | Final AI Project          |

The same structure is maintained across Years 3–9 while increasing
complexity and independence.

This consistency helps learners develop familiarity with the curriculum
model while supporting progression.
