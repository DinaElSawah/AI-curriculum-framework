# Year 3

### Year at a Glance

| **Component**         | **Description**                                                       |
|-----------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Big Idea**          | Artificial Intelligence is all around us and responds to information. |
| **Progression Stage** | Exploration                                                           |

| **Key Technologies**  | ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Google Teachable Machine                      |
| **Assessment Focus**  | Recognition, explanation, participation and responsible use           |

### Learning Focus

Year 3 establishes the foundations of Artificial Intelligence by
introducing learners to AI technologies encountered in everyday life.
Through guided exploration and practical interaction, learners begin
recognising AI-enabled systems, identifying simple patterns within data,
and understanding that AI systems respond to information rather than
think like humans. Learners interact purposefully with AI tools using
simple prompts while developing an awareness of safe, responsible, and
ethical AI use. The year provides the conceptual foundation for future
learning in machine learning, prompt engineering, and intelligent
systems.

## AI Recognition & Understanding (RU)

**Intent**

This domain develops learners’ awareness of Artificial Intelligence by
introducing AI technologies encountered in everyday life. Learners begin
recognising the characteristics of AI-enabled systems and develop a
simple understanding of how AI systems receive information and generate
responses.

**Enduring Understanding**

Artificial Intelligence systems receive information, identify patterns,
and generate responses using data.

**Essential Question**

**How do AI systems know what to do?**

| **Code**      | **Learning Objective**                                                                                        | **Suggested Evidence**              | **UNESCO** | **ISTE** |
|---------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------|------------|----------|
| **3.AI.RU.1** | Identify examples of artificial intelligence technologies encountered in everyday life.                       | Discussion, Observation             | U3         | I3       |
| **3.AI.RU.2** | Identify the differences between AI-enabled systems and technologies that do not use artificial intelligence. | Practical Task                      | U3         | I3, I5   |
| **3.AI.RU.3** | Describe, using simple language, how AI systems receive information and generate responses.                   | Oral Explanation, Annotated Diagram | U3, U6     | I3       |

**Intent**

This domain introduces learners to safe, respectful, and responsible
interaction with AI technologies. Learners begin recognising that AI
should be used responsibly and that personal information should be

**Enduring Understanding**

Artificial Intelligence should always be used safely and responsibly.

| **3.AI.ES.2** | Explain why personal information should not be shared with AI systems.          | Role-play, Discussion  | U2, U5     | I2       |

### End of Year Expectations

By the end of **Year 3**, learners should be able to identify common AI
technologies encountered in everyday life, distinguish AI-enabled
systems from non-AI technologies, describe in simple terms how AI
systems receive information and generate responses, identify patterns
within simple datasets, recognise that AI systems learn from examples,
construct simple prompts to interact with AI tools, recognise how
prompts influence AI-generated responses, and demonstrate safe and
responsible behaviours when using Artificial Intelligence technologies.

### Preparing for Year 4

Learners completing Year 3 are prepared to:

- explain how AI systems identify patterns and make simple decisions;

- classify information using labelled examples;

- refine prompts to improve AI-generated responses;

- compare the capabilities of different AI systems;

- explore fairness and accuracy in simple AI applications.

### Learning Focus

Year 4 builds upon the foundational understanding established in Year 3
by developing learners’ ability to explain how Artificial Intelligence
systems recognise patterns, classify information, and support
decision-making. Learners investigate how data influences AI systems and
begin comparing different AI applications based on their purpose and
capabilities. Through increasingly purposeful interaction with AI tools,
learners refine their prompts to improve AI-generated responses and
explore how responsible human decisions influence the safe and ethical
use of Artificial Intelligence. The year prepares learners to apply AI
concepts more independently in Year 5 through structured investigations
and practical problem-solving.

### End of Year Expectations

By the end of **Year 4**, learners should be able to explain how
Artificial Intelligence systems recognise patterns and perform specific
tasks, compare different AI applications based on their purpose,
classify information using simple machine learning models, explain how
training examples influence AI performance, construct and refine prompts
to improve AI-generated responses, and demonstrate responsible
decision-making by recognising the importance of checking AI-generated
information and protecting personal data.

### Preparing for Year 5

Learners completing Year 4 are prepared to:

- investigate how machine learning models are trained using datasets;

- apply AI tools to solve structured problems;

- construct prompts for different purposes and audiences;

- evaluate the strengths and limitations of AI-generated responses;

- recognise how bias and data quality influence AI performance.
