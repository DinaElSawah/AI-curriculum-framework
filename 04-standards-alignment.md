# UNESCO AI Competency Framework Alignment

## 7.1 Purpose of Alignment

The Artificial Intelligence Curriculum Framework (Years 3–9) has been
designed to align with internationally recognised approaches to AI
education and competency development.

The framework draws upon the principles of the UNESCO AI Competency
Framework for Students while adapting them to the developmental needs of
learners in Years 3–9.

The alignment supports the development of learners who can:

- understand how AI systems operate;

- interact effectively with AI technologies;

- evaluate AI-generated outputs critically;

- apply AI responsibly and ethically;

- contribute positively to an increasingly AI-enabled society.

Rather than adopting UNESCO competencies as standalone units, the
curriculum embeds these competencies throughout all four curriculum
domains and across all stages of progression.

This integrated approach ensures that AI knowledge, practical
application, ethical awareness, and critical evaluation develop
simultaneously.

## 7.2 UNESCO Alignment Philosophy

The curriculum reflects three overarching principles that underpin
UNESCO’s approach to AI education.

### Human-Centred Development

Learners are encouraged to understand AI as a technology designed and
controlled by people rather than as an autonomous replacement for human
judgement.

### Ethical and Responsible Participation

Learners develop awareness of the responsibilities associated with
creating, using, and evaluating AI systems.

### Future-Oriented Competency Development

Learners develop competencies that support lifelong learning,
adaptability, innovation, and participation in future technological
environments.

# ISTE Standards Alignment

## 8.1 Purpose of Alignment

The Artificial Intelligence Curriculum Framework (Years 3–9) aligns with
the ISTE Standards for Students by supporting learners in becoming
empowered, responsible, creative, and reflective users of technology.

The curriculum promotes not only technical competence but also the
skills, attitudes, and behaviours required to use emerging technologies
effectively and ethically.

Through practical interaction with AI systems, project-based learning,
critical evaluation, and ethical inquiry, learners progressively develop
the competencies described within the ISTE Standards.

Rather than teaching ISTE standards as separate objectives, the
framework embeds them throughout all four curriculum domains and across
all stages of progression.

This approach ensures that AI learning supports broader digital
competencies while maintaining a clear focus on artificial intelligence.

## 8.2 ISTE Standards Overview

The curriculum aligns with the seven ISTE Student Standards:

| **ISTE Standard**     | **Description**                                                                                            |
|-----------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Empowered Learner     | Learners use technology to take an active role in achieving learning goals.                                |
| Digital Citizen       | Learners recognise the rights, responsibilities, and opportunities associated with technology use.         |
| Knowledge Constructor | Learners critically evaluate information and build knowledge using digital tools.                          |
| Innovative Designer   | Learners use technology to design, test, and improve solutions.                                            |
| Computational Thinker | Learners develop strategies for understanding and solving problems using data and computational processes. |
| Creative Communicator | Learners communicate ideas effectively using appropriate digital tools.                                    |
| Global Collaborator   | Learners use technology to work with others and contribute to shared learning experiences.                 |

the societal implications of emerging technologies.

## 8.7 ISTE Alignment Summary

The Artificial Intelligence Curriculum Framework demonstrates strong
alignment with the ISTE Standards for Students by embedding digital
citizenship, computational thinking, innovation, communication, and
responsible technology use throughout all stages of learning.

The framework ensures that learners not only develop AI-specific
knowledge and skills but also acquire broader competencies that support

increasingly digital world.
