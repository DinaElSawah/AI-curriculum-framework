# AI Curriculum Framework (Years 3–9)

A complete, standards-aligned curriculum for teaching Artificial Intelligence as an AI strand within a STEAM programme — developed as a **STEM.org Certified™ educational programme**, informed by the **UNESCO AI Competency Framework for Students** and the **ISTE Standards for Students**, with an original progression model spanning Years 3 through 9.

This is the instructional-design piece of the portfolio: where the [AI Enablement Playbook](https://github.com/DinaElSawah/ai-enablement-playbook) is about teaching *staff* to use AI, this framework is about teaching *students* to understand, question, and responsibly apply it.

## The four learning domains

- **AI Recognition & Understanding (RU)** — how AI systems work and where they show up in everyday life
- **Machine Learning & Data (ML)** — how models learn from data, tested and refined through increasing use of Python
- **Prompt Engineering & AI Interaction (PE)** — purposeful, iterative interaction with AI systems
- **Ethics & Societal Impact (ES)** — fairness, transparency, privacy, intellectual property, and responsible innovation

Each domain runs across all seven years on a spiral progression model — revisited with increasing complexity rather than taught once and left behind — moving learners from **Exploration** (Years 3–4) through **Interaction** (Years 5–6) and **Design** (Years 7–8) to **Evaluation** (Year 9).

## Why this approach

A curriculum that only teaches students to *use* AI tools is already out of date by the time they graduate. This framework instead asks students to investigate how AI systems are built, trained, evaluated, and improved — so the understanding transfers even as the specific tools change. Ethics isn't a separate unit bolted onto the end; it's embedded in every domain, every year.
